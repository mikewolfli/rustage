# Code of Conduct

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, project discussions,
and public interactions when representing rustage.

## Expectations

- Respect different experience levels, languages, backgrounds, and opinions.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- State limitations and uncertainty honestly.
- Accept constructive feedback and help new contributors understand the project.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, disclosure of personal
information, spam, repeated disruption, and impersonating maintainers are not
acceptable. Technical disagreement does not justify abuse or retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request edits, remove inappropriate content, restrict discussions,
or suspend participation. Decisions consider impact, severity, and repetition,
while protecting the privacy of affected people.

Participants may request reconsideration. A maintainer with a conflict of interest
should avoid deciding the matter alone when another suitable reviewer is available.

## Reporting and reconsideration

The repository owner, [@mikewolfli](https://github.com/mikewolfli), handles initial
reports. Use GitHub's reporting features for inappropriate public content.
Non-sensitive process questions may use the “Design and repository governance”
issue form to request maintainer involvement.

For private matters, use GitHub's reporting channels or a private contact
explicitly published on the maintainer's profile. The project has not yet
published a dedicated conduct-reporting email address.
Do not post identities, private evidence, or sensitive details in public issues
to compensate for a missing private channel.

This is the project's own community policy, not a claim that a dedicated
private reporting service has already been deployed.
